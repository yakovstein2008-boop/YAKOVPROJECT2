## The repeatable recipe — find 50 more in an afternoon

This is how you generate an endless supply of **Tier A** prospects (the invisible, no-website
ones that convert best). About 27% of U.S. small businesses still have no website — they're out there.

### Method 1 — Google Maps "no website" sweep (free, best)
1. Open Google Maps. Search a trade + area: `pressure washing near Round Rock TX`,
   `handyman Pflugerville`, `mobile detailing Cedar Park`, `landscaping Georgetown TX`.
2. Click each listing. **If there's no "Website" button** (or it points to Facebook/Instagram/
   Linktree) → that's a lead.
3. Grab: business name, owner name if shown, phone, and the FB/listing URL. Drop it in `prospects.csv`.
4. Best trades to target (often no site): pressure washing, mobile detailing, junk removal,
   handyman, house cleaning, lawn care, mobile mechanics, fencing, pool cleaning, pest control,
   moving, hauling, tree work.

### Method 2 — Google search operators
- `site:facebook.com "pressure washing" "Austin"` → businesses living on Facebook = no site.
- `"plumber" "Austin" -site:* ` style searches, or add `"call or text"` (a tell of no website).
- `intext:"find us on facebook" {{trade}} {{city}}`.

### Method 3 — New business filings (great for the "congrats" Template #6)
- Texas Comptroller / Secretary of State new business & DBA filings, or sites like
  OpenCorporates, list brand-new LLCs. New business = a perfect trigger event to pitch.

### Method 4 — Directories with weak-web businesses
- Yelp, Thumbtack, Angi, Nextdoor, Yellow Pages, Craigslist services, local Facebook groups,
  Chamber of Commerce member lists. Filter for listings with **no website link** or only a social link.

### Method 5 — Automate it (when you're ready to scale)
- Google-Maps "businesses without websites" scrapers: **Trovn, Targetron, Outscraper, Apify**.
  They output a CSV of no-website businesses with phone + category in minutes.

### Qualify each lead (30 seconds)
Keep it only if: ✅ no site or a clearly weak/dated one · ✅ it's a local service business
(they live or die by local search) · ✅ you can identify the owner/decision-maker · ✅ they look
active (recent reviews/posts). Then pick the template and log it.
