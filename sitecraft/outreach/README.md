## The one number that matters

Across the data: **the first email gets ~37% of replies, but follow-ups bring the other ~55%+.**
Most people who win at cold email simply follow up 3–4 times. If you do nothing else
from this kit, do that.

## 5-minute quick start

1. **Set up your email** — see `01`. Cheapest credible option: one Gmail
   (`sitecraft.studio.tx@gmail.com` or similar). Best option: a real domain +
   Google Workspace. Don't mass-send on day one.
2. **Pick 10 prospects** — start with the **Tier A** businesses in `03` (the ones with
   no real website — your easiest wins).
3. **Pick Template 1** ("I already made you something") from `02`. It's the strongest opener.
4. **Spend 60 seconds per prospect** finding ONE true detail to personalize (a review, their
   FB page, "no website," a service they push). Drop it into the `{{specific_observation}}` slot.
5. **Send 5–10/day, by hand, Tue–Thu mornings.** Don't blast 50 on a cold inbox — you'll
   land in spam. Ramp up over 2–3 weeks (see `01`).
6. **Follow up** on Day 3, Day 10, Day 17 using the sequence in `02`. Track every send in `prospects.csv`.

## Realistic expectations

- Good B2B cold-email reply rates run **5–10%**. On 50 well-targeted, personalized emails,
  expect a handful of replies and 1–3 real conversations.
- Your free-preview offer should beat those averages because the ask is tiny and the value is concrete.
- This is a numbers + persistence game. The kit is built so you can run it consistently.
